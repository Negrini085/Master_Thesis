\section{Thesis outline}

This thesis is structured as follows:
\begin{itemize}[label=$\blacktriangleright$]
    \item \textit{Chapter \ref{cap:snow_basics}} provides the theoretical background needed to understand the work presented in this thesis. The 
    first part focuses on snow physics: it starts from the nucleation of ice crystals within cold clouds and follows the snowflakes down to the 
    ground, where they accumulate into a new layer. It then describes snow metamorphism and the energy balance of the snowpack, and finally addresses 
    liquid water percolation and runoff generation. The second part is devoted to snow measurement techniques, distinguishing between manual 
    observations, automatic monitoring networks and remote sensing, and discussing the advantages and limitations of each approach.
\end{itemize}
